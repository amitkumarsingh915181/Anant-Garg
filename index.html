THE STORY

Beyond the Lens

I didn’t begin with a plan — just curiosity. At first, I experimented with video editing through simple hit-and-trial, exploring what worked and what didn’t. There was no structure, just a genuine interest in understanding how visuals come together. Over time, that curiosity slowly turned into a passion.

My journey truly began with CapCut, where I started creating and learning the basics. As I kept exploring, I became more fascinated by the power of storytelling through visuals — how even a single frame can shift perception, evoke emotion, and tell a story without words.

Driven by this curiosity, I moved towards more advanced tools like Adobe Premiere Pro and Adobe After Effects. Today, I don’t just edit videos — I craft visual experiences. And it all started with one simple question: how can a single frame change the way we see something?
